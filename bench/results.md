# IV solver benchmark results

Generated by `python bench/benchmark.py --sizes 1000,10000,100000 --repeats 20 --python-repeats 20,20,3`. Raw numbers are in `results.json`.

**Read with `results_scipy118.md`.** These results used NumPy 2.4.4 and SciPy 1.17.1. With SciPy 1.18.1 the NumPy backend is 1.14x to 1.18x *faster* than C++ at 10k and 100k contracts, because `scipy.special.ndtr` became 7.3x faster (`bench/kernels.py`).

## Results

Time to solve IV for the whole chain once. Lower is better. Speedup is relative to the per-row python backend (median vs median).

| Contracts | Backend | Runs | Median | p95 | Throughput | Speedup vs python | Speedup vs numpy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | python | 20 | 3.88 s | 5.09 s | 258 /s | 1x | 0.00637x |
| 1,000 | numpy | 20 | 24.73 ms | 57.71 ms | 40.4 k/s | 157x | 1x |
| 1,000 | cpp | 20 | 4.35 ms | 5.30 ms | 229.8 k/s | 892x | 5.68x |
| 10,000 | python | 20 | 46.88 s | 48.39 s | 213 /s | 1x | 0.00444x |
| 10,000 | numpy | 20 | 208.07 ms | 328.77 ms | 48.1 k/s | 225x | 1x |
| 10,000 | cpp | 20 | 35.11 ms | 42.00 ms | 284.9 k/s | 1,335x | 5.93x |
| 100,000 | python | 3 | 533.12 s | 701.77 s | 188 /s | 1x | 0.00317x |
| 100,000 | numpy | 20 | 1.69 s | 1.81 s | 59.2 k/s | 316x | 1x |
| 100,000 | cpp | 20 | 354.08 ms | 361.03 ms | 282.4 k/s | 1,506x | 4.77x |

Share of contracts where Newton gave up and the bracketing fallback ran: 39.0% (same in every backend, same algorithm).
The per-row python backend at the largest size has fewer timed runs (each run takes several minutes), so its p95 is close to its maximum and is less stable than the other rows.
